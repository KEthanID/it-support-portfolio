# Framework reference

Use this page to connect a short write-up to the skills behind it. Pick a few items you can explain through your own work and add a short reason in the write-up's expandable **Framework connections** section. Your instructor can help with this part.

The identifiers and descriptions below preserve the list supplied for this portfolio, including **NICE Framework Components v2.0.0**. Connections in the examples are suggested educational mappings. They describe the part practiced in that entry, rather than a proficiency rating or complete coverage of a Knowledge Unit.

For example, the [printer write-up](write-ups/printer-offline.md) connects configuring and testing a printer to **DCWF 221A** and **NICE S0679/S0680**. A research or remote-access mapping would need an entry that actually describes that work.
